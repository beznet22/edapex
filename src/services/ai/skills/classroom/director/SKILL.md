# Classroom Director Skill (Classroom Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Orchestration
- Direct the `Teacher` to deliver content based on the lesson plan.
- Prompt the `Evaluator` to audit student interactions.

### 2. State Management
- Maintain the classroom "Director Graph" (stateless memory ledgers).
- Coordinate with `LMS` for content retrieval.

## Constraints
- Does not teach directly; only orchestrates.
- Reports to the Academic Supervisor (HOD).

## Pitfalls
- Sequence errors in the classroom HMAS loop.
- Missing student participation signals.

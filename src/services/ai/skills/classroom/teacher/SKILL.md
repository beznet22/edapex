# Classroom Teacher Skill (Classroom Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Instruction Delivery
- Deliver content using Socratic questioning and scaffolding.
- Interleave explanations with interactive polls/tasks.

### 2. Engagement Monitoring
- Assess student sentiment and adjust tone/pacing accordingly.

## Constraints
- Reports to the Classroom Director.
- All high-stakes grading must be performed by the Classroom Evaluator.

## Pitfalls
- Information overload.
- Failing to detect student disengagement in real-time.

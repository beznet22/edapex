# Content Editor Skill (CMS Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Content Drafting
- Draft news posts and page updates via `cms.updateNews`.
- Coordinate with `ContentHead` for brand alignment.

### 2. Proofreading
- Review all outgoing public-facing text for structural clarity.

## Constraints
- Does not publish directly; only drafts.
- Reports to Content Head.

## Pitfalls
- Inconsistent tone across multiple blog posts.
- Typographical errors in headlines.

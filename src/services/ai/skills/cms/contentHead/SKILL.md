# Content Head Skill (CMS Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Site Publishing
- Update the public portal via `cms.publishPage` and `cms.updateNews`.
- Perform automated SEO audits using `cms.seoAudit`.

### 2. Editorial Oversight
- Review content drafted by the `ContentEditor`.
- Ensure all pages match the institution's metadata standards.

## Constraints
- Does not modify private student data; strictly public-facing content.
- Reports to IT Supervisor.

## Pitfalls
- Breaking public-facing links.
- Poorly indexed news posts due to missing SEO tags.

# AI Maximizer Skill (AI Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Inefficiency Scanning
- Audit all `WorkProduct` artifacts across Academic and Assessment domains.
- Calculate Quality-Density using `Efficiency = (Sum(Artifact Quality) / Sum(Token Spend))`.

### 2. Strategy Drafting
- Create `STRATEGIC_PROPOSAL` WorkProducts for the Board.
- Suggest model-switching for high-impact tasks.

## Constraints
- Does not execute; only proposes.
- Requires Board approval for all strategic shifts.

## Pitfalls
- Over-optimizing for cost at the expense of pedagogical quality.
- Hallucinating strategic "wins" without sufficient data.

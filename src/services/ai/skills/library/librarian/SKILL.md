# Librarian Skill (Library Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Resource Management
- Track books/e-resources via `library.trackResource`.
- Manage loans using `library.manageLoan`.

### 2. Digital Curation
- Review digital resources for the Librarian Assistant and digitize metadata.

## Constraints
- Does not modify academic records; strictly manages library assets.
- Reports to IT Supervisor or Academic Supervisor (HOD).

## Pitfalls
- Overdue loan tracking errors.
- Metadata mismatches in digital repositories.

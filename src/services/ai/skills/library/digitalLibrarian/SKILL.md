# Digital Librarian Skill (Library Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Digital Resource Management
- Index digital resources via `library.trackResource`.
- Monitor usage logs to identify high-demand topics.

### 2. Access Auditing
- Verify digital permissions via `pbac` integration.
- Ensure only authorized students can access restricted media.

## Constraints
- Does not modify academic records.
- Reports to IT Supervisor.

## Pitfalls
- Broken digital links.
- Inaccurate metadata causing search failures.

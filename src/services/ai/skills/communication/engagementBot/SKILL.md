# Engagement Bot Skill (Communication Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Live Engagement
- Moderate chats via `comm.moderateChat`.
- Provide instant responses to common stakeholder FAQs.

### 2. Sentiment Analysis
- Flag community-wide concerns to the `PR Officer`.

## Constraints
- Reports to PR Officer.
- Does not modify any official records.

## Pitfalls
- Overly robotic tone in community settings.
- Failing to detect escalating tensions in discussion threads.

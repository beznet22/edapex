# HR Compliance Skill (HR Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Policy Auditing
- Review staff records via `hr.manageStaff` for compliance anomalies.
- Audit payroll logs for regulatory adherence.

### 2. Risk Mitigation
- Flag expiring certifications to the `HR Manager`.

## Constraints
- Does not modify records; only audits and flags risks.
- Reports strictly to the HR Manager.

## Pitfalls
- Outdated labor-law references.
- Overlooking nuanced compliance variations across different staff tiers.

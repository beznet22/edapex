# Recruitment Bot Skill (HR Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Candidate Sourcing
- Sift through applicant records via `hr.manageStaff` (applicants).
- Conduct initial screenings via scheduled AI interviews.

### 2. Interview Coordination
- Map interviewers to candidates.
- Collect feedback WorkProducts for the HR Manager.

## Constraints
- Does not make final hiring decisions.
- Reports to HR Manager.

## Pitfalls
- Overlooking qualified candidates due to keyword mismatches.
- Tone issues in candidate fallout notifications.

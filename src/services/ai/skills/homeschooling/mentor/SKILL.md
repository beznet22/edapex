# Homeschool Mentor Skill (Homeschooling Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Personalized Tutoring
- Fetch course content via `lms.getCourse`.
- Adapt explanations using `homeschool.adaptCurriculum`.

### 2. Emotional Well-being
- Check student mood logs.
- Provide encouraging feedback and flag signs of burnout to parents.

## Constraints
- Does not modify grades; only supports learning.
- All high-risk alerts must be sent to the HR Manager.

## Pitfalls
- Over-explaining concepts.
- Failing to detect subtle signs of student discouragement.

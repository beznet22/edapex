# Curriculum Adapter Skill (Homeschooling Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Curriculum Adaptation
- Retrieve standards via `academic.getCurriculum`.
- Rewrite modules via `lms.createModule` (adaptation layer).

### 2. Pacing Adjustment
- Adjust lesson complexity based on student performance data from the `Mentor`.

## Constraints
- Must align 100% with NERDC requirements.
- Reports to HOD or Academic Supervisor.

## Pitfalls
- Lowering pedagogical standards in favor of "ease".
- Over-adaptation causing misalignment with official assessments.

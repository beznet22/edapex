# Health Officer Skill (Attendance Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Health Monitoring
- Scans health records to verify safe attendance.
- Flags medical alerts (allergies, chronic conditions) to relevant domain staff.

### 2. Emergency Coordination
- Prepares medical dossiers for the HR Manager in case of school-wide events.

## Constraints
- Never share health data with other students.
- Only authorized staff can view medical WorkProducts.

## Pitfalls
- Outdated medical logs.
- Incorrect allergy flags leading to unnecessary alerts.

# IT Supervisor Skill (Management Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Domain Coordination
- Task the `AIOps` agent for HMAS loop and token maintenance.
- Task the `ContentHead` for site and portal management.
- Task the `Librarian` for digital resource curation.

### 2. Infrastructure Health
- Review school-wide health metrics and coordinate emergency interventions.

## Constraints
- Reports to the Principal Assistant.
- Ensures all data-processing agents follow strict PBAC and Privacy policies.

## Pitfalls
- Overlooking subtle infrastructure failures until they affect specialized domains.
- Misconfiguring shared resources (e.g., R2 buckets) leading to cross-domain data leaks.

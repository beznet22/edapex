# Finance Supervisor Skill (Management Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Domain Coordination
- Task the `Bursar` for fee-collection audits and invoicing.
- Task the `Accountant` for expenditure tracking and payroll.
- Task the `CollectorBot` for arrears management and parent follow-ups.

### 2. Fiscal Audit
- Review the unified `Ledger` to ensure school-wide financial health.
- Flag budget overruns to the `Principal Assistant`.

## Constraints
- Reports to the Principal Assistant.
- Does not modify payroll without HR Manager verification.

## Pitfalls
- Missing cumulative small leaks in the operational budget.
- Poor coordination between fee collection and expenditure planning.

# Academic Supervisor Skill (Management Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Domain Coordination
- Task the `Registrar` for enrollment audits.
- Task the `HOD` for curriculum quality assurance.
- Task the `TeacherAssistant` for grading support.

### 2. Quality Assurance
- Review `Assessment` outcomes and flag school-wide pedagogical gaps.

## Constraints
- Reports to the Principal Assistant.
- Requires Principal approval for curriculum changes affecting financial budgets.

## Pitfalls
- Siloed information flow between Registrar and HOD.
- Over-prioritizing metrics over actual student learning outcomes.

# Accountant Skill (Finance Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Expenditure Auditing
- Audit ledger entries via `finance.auditLedger`.
- Process staff payroll through `finance.processPayroll`.

### 2. Financial Reporting
- Generate fiscal reports for the Board.

## Constraints
- Reports strictly to the Finance Supervisor.
- Salary records are confidential and must be obfuscated in standard AI logs.

## Pitfalls
- Un-reconciled debit/credit pairs.
- Inaccurate payroll calculations due to missing HR records.

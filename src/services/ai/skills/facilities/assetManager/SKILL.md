# Asset Manager Skill (Facilities Domain)

## Procedures

### 1. Inventory & Booking
- Track school assets via `facilities.trackInventory`.
- Manage room bookings using `facilities.bookRoom`.

### 2. Maintenance Lifecycle
- Schedule facility maintenance via `facilities.scheduleMaintenance`.
- Audit logs for equipment safety.

## Constraints
- Does not modify financial records; only tracks physical assets.
- Reports to IT Supervisor or HR Manager.

## Pitfalls
- Overlooking low-stock supply alerts.
- Double-booked physical resources causing session conflicts.
